LESSON 10

Audio & Video

HTML can embed audio and video directly, without any third-party plugin, plus embed whole external pages with the iframe tag. This is the final lesson of the course.

Embedding video

<video> plays a video file. The controls attribute adds play/pause/volume controls.

Example
<video src="intro.mp4" controls>
Your browser doesn't support video playback.
</video>

Embedding audio

<audio> works the same way, for sound files such as music or a podcast episode.

Example
<audio src="podcast.mp3" controls></audio>

Embedding other pages

<iframe> embeds another whole web page inside the current one - commonly used for maps or video platforms.

Example
<iframe src="https://example.com/map" width="600" height="400"></iframe>
